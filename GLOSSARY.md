# Duyfken Sail-Handling Learning

Terminology for learning sail-handling procedures on the Duyfken replica.

## Language

**Duyfken**:
The replica vessel whose rig and sail-handling procedures are the subject of this learning aid, rather than the original historical vessel.
_Avoid_: Generic square-rigged vessel

**Main course**:
The lower square sail on Duyfken's mainmast, distinct from the yard that carries it.
_Avoid_: Course yard

**Course yard**:
The spar that carries a course sail; in the main course exercise, this means the main course yard.
_Avoid_: Main course sail

**Main topsail**:
The square sail above the main course on Duyfken's mainmast, distinct from the yard that carries it.
_Avoid_: Top yard

**Main topsail yard**:
The spar that carries the main topsail.
_Avoid_: Furled yard

**Unfurled**:
The state of a sail that is no longer gathered and secured in its furled form; this does not by itself mean the sail is set.
_Avoid_: Loose

**Leech**:
A side edge of the main course sail, distinct from its bottom edge.
_Avoid_: Leach, foot

**Training material**:
Vessel-specific instructional material used as a source for Duyfken's sail-handling procedures.
_Avoid_: Generic historical descriptions

**Qualified reviewer**:
A person qualified to teach sail handling on Duyfken who reviews the learning aid's procedures for accuracy.

**Learner**:
A beginner preparing for supervised training on Duyfken, with no assumed prior square-rig experience.
_Avoid_: Qualified crew member

**Command**:
A sail-handling instruction whose procedure the learner must demonstrate for stated starting conditions.

**Step**:
An action within a sail-handling procedure, identifying the relevant lines where applicable.

**Approved ordering**:
An arrangement of steps accepted by a qualified reviewer, including any required dependencies or coordinated actions.
_Avoid_: The only correct sequence

**Coordinated actions**:
Actions that must be performed together, rather than completed as separate sequential steps.

**Draft procedure**:
A proposed sail-handling procedure that has not yet been approved by a qualified reviewer.
_Avoid_: Approved procedure
